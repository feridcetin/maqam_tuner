**Full description**
Maqam Tuner shows what a normal tuner cannot: quarter tones and Turkish komas.

• Three tuning systems: 12 (Western), 24 (Arabic quarter tones) and 53 (Turkish koma)
• Chromatic mode: nearest note, cents and Hz in real time
• Instrument mode: four oud tunings (Arabic and Turkish), Guitar, Violin and more. Tap a string to lock it
• Scale practice: Rast, Bayati, Hijaz, Uşşak and more. See the nearest scale degree and how far you are
• No ads while you tune
• Reference tones: hear any step of any system, steady or plucked
• Adjustable A4 from 415 to 466 Hz
• Works offline. Audio is analysed on your phone and never recorded

Free to use. Go Pro once for extra instruments, scales, custom tunings and no ads, or remove ads with a single payment. No forced subscription.
