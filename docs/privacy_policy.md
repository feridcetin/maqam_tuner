# Privacy Policy — Maqam Tuner

Last updated: [DATE]

**Microphone.** Maqam Tuner uses your microphone only to measure the pitch of the sound you play. Audio is analysed
in real time on your device. It is not recorded, stored, or sent anywhere.

**Ads.** The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID and
related data to show ads and measure performance. In regions where required (EEA, UK), you are asked for consent, and you can
change your choice later in Settings > Ad privacy choices. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

**Purchases.** Payments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details.

**Data we store.** Your settings (reference pitch, tuning system, selected instrument) are stored locally on your device.
We do not run servers and do not collect personal information.

**Children.** The app is not directed to children under 13.
